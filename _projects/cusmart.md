## What it does

CUSMART is a CUDA-based implementation of string-matching algorithms. It provides a framework for evaluating parallel exact string matching and studying how algorithmic choices affect GPU performance.

## My contributions

At Hacettepe University, I parallelized around 20 string-matching algorithms using GPGPU accelerators. I contributed to CUSMART and co-authored the study evaluating the resulting framework.

## Related research

**CUSMART: effective parallelization of string matching algorithms using GPGPU accelerators** appeared in _Frontiers of Information Technology & Electronic Engineering_ in 2025.
