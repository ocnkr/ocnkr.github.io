## What it does

Pipit supports programmatic analysis of parallel execution traces from multiple tracing tools. Its pandas-based interface enables researchers to aggregate, filter, and transform trace events and build reproducible analysis workflows.

## My contributions

As a developer and maintainer of Pipit, I focused on developing analysis features, including pattern detection in execution traces. I also mentored students working on Pipit and co-authored the research describing its programmatic approach to trace analysis.

## Related research

**Pipit: Scripting the analysis of parallel execution traces** describes a uniform trace representation and extensible analysis methods. Earlier work was presented as a research poster at SC 2023.
